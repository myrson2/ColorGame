# Color Game Website

A vibrant and interactive web-based color betting game built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Players can set their balance, place bets on multiple colors, and experience the thrill of the "Roll It!" animated roulette.

## 🚀 Features

- **Dynamic Balance Management:** Set your starting balance and track your wins and losses in real-time.
- **Quick Bet Options:** Easily select bet amounts (5, 10, 50, 100) for fast-paced gameplay.
- **Six Betting Colors:** Place bets on Red, Green, Purple, Yellow, Blue, and Pink.
- **Animated Roulette:** Visual feedback with a rolling color animation when the game starts.
- **Detailed Result Summary:** A comprehensive modal that breaks down your bets per color, identifies wins and losses, and calculates your new total balance.
- **Responsive & Modern UI:** A sleek, dark-themed interface with glassmorphism effects and smooth transitions.
- **Persistent Gameplay:** Choose to "Bet Again" with your remaining balance or "Cashout" to restart.

## 🎮 How to Play

1.  **Launch the Game:** Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
2.  **Initial Setup:**
    - Enter your starting balance (must be more than 100).
    - Select a "Quick Bet" amount from the grid.
    - Click **Save & Play**.
3.  **Place Your Bets:** Click on the color tiles in the "Place Bet" section. Each click adds your selected bet amount to that color.
4.  **Roll It!:** Once you've placed your bets, click the **Roll It!** button.
5.  **View Results:** After the 3-second animation, a result modal will appear showing the winning colors and your total payout.
6.  **Continue or Reset:** Click **Bet Again** to play another round or **Cashout** to finish.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5:** Semantic structure of the game UI.
- **CSS3:** Custom styling, layout (Flexbox/Grid), and animations. Features Google Fonts (Funnel Sans).
- **JavaScript (ES6+):** Core game logic, state management, and DOM manipulation.
